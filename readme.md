# Tartu IOT Bike

## Story

A cyclist recently got a new job and wants to start commuting by bike. However, his new office is in a bad neighborhood and he is afraid his bike will get stolen. Next to his office, there are 2 smart bike racks that are harder to cut through than a cheap lock.

He signs up for the application and is sent a RFID tag that he can use to lock and unlock the bike rack. He can also see the status of the bike rack online to make sure there are spaces available before he arrives. At one point he sees a bunch of kids trying to lock the bike rack with no bikes inside, however the bike rack only works with authenticated tags and does not allow locking without a bike inside by verifying the presence of a bike with an ultrasonic sensor.
